Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Overline ("Overline", "the app") is a Windows desktop app. It runs entirely on your computer. There is no Overline server and no Overline account: we, the developers, never receive, store or see your data. This policy explains what the app reads, where that data stays, and when anything leaves your computer.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose "Continue with Google", Overline asks only for the access below, and only when you turn the matching feature on (Gmail and Classroom are separate switches in Settings → Calendar).

Google permission What Overline reads or does Why
Your email address (openid, email) The address of the account you signed in with. To show which account is connected in Settings.
Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly) Upcoming events: title, start and end time, location, video-meeting link, and the event's Google Calendar link. Nothing is ever created, changed or deleted. To show your next meeting in the notch, remind you before it starts, and offer a one-click "Join" button.
Google Calendar events (calendar.events) — only if you turn on Add events and answer invites Creates an event you typed and clicked to add, and sets your own Yes / Maybe / No answer on an invite you answered (Google tells the organizer, as Google Calendar does). Overline never deletes events and never changes anything you didn't click. To add events and answer invites from the notch or from Quick capture.
Gmail (gmail.modify) — only if you turn on Gmail For unread messages in your inbox: sender, subject, Gmail's short preview snippet and date. When you click "Archive" or "Mark as read" on a message in the notch, Overline removes that one message's INBOX or UNREAD label. Snoozing a message hides it in Overline until the time you picked; only its message id and that time are kept on your PC, and nothing changes in Gmail. Overline never reads full message bodies or attachments, never sends, deletes or drafts mail, and never changes settings. To show your unread count and newest senders, notify you of new mail, and let you archive or mark a message as read from the notch.
Google Classroom courses, read-only (classroom.courses.readonly) The names of your active courses. To label your deadlines with their course.
Your Classroom coursework, read-only (classroom.coursework.me.readonly) Assignment titles, due dates, links, and whether you have already turned them in. To remind you of work that is due soon and hide what you've already submitted.

Where Google data is stored

Google data is fetched directly from Google's servers by the app on your PC. Calendar events are kept in a local database on your computer so the notch works offline, and a day plan you asked for (which can mention meeting titles) is kept there until the next one replaces it. Mail details and Classroom deadlines are kept only in memory while the app runs. Your Google sign-in token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service.

When Google data is shared

Google data is not shared with anyone, with one opt-in exception: the AI day planner. If you turn on Share Google data with the planner (Settings → Home, off by default), then when a day plan is made, today's meeting titles and times and your Classroom deadlines (titles, course names and due dates) are sent to the AI service you chose in Settings → AI (for example Claude Code by Anthropic, Codex by OpenAI, Gemini CLI by Google, a local Ollama model, or an API you configured), solely to write that plan. Gmail data is never sent to any AI service. You can turn this off at any time.

Limited Use

Overline's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Overline:

  • uses Google user data only to provide the features described above, which you can see in the app;
  • does not sell Google user data, and does not use or transfer it for advertising, credit-worthiness, or lending;
  • does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models;
  • does not let any human read your Google data. The developers have no access to it at all, because it never leaves your computer except as described above.

Removing access and deleting data

In Settings → Calendar, Disconnect revokes Overline's access at Google, deletes your sign-in token, and deletes the cached calendar events and day plan from your computer; mail and Classroom details are dropped from memory. You can also remove Overline's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Overline removes the app; its local data folder can be deleted from %APPDATA%\com.notchdesktop.app and %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.notchdesktop.app.

Other data the app uses

These features also run on your PC; the only outside services they contact are listed here.

  • Spotify (optional sign-in): your playlists and playback, from Spotify's API.
  • GitHub (optional sign-in): pull requests and notifications, from GitHub's API.
  • Weather: your approximate location (from Windows location services, if you allow it) is sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast and to BigDataCloud for the place name.
  • Lyrics (optional): the title and artist of the song playing are sent to LRCLIB.
  • Ask and AI features: questions you type, an optional screenshot of the window you choose, your open tasks, and code changes for commit messages go to the AI service you chose in Settings → AI. When you click Let AI sort it in Quick capture, the line you typed goes to it; when you click Summarize on a saved link, that article's text goes to it.
  • Read later: when you save a link, Overline opens that page once to read its title and length. Only the link, title, site name, word count and any summary are kept on your PC.
  • Speed test (when you run it): test data is downloaded from and uploaded to Cloudflare's speed test (speed.cloudflare.com). The "apps using the internet" list is read from Windows on your PC and never leaves it.
  • Clipboard, screen time, to-do, focus, password vault: stored only on your computer. Passwords and secrets are kept in Windows Credential Manager or your own encrypted Obsidian vault.

Overline contains no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no tracking.

Children

Overline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
