A Dynamic Island for Windows

Notch Desktop is a small bar at the top of your screen that shows what matters right now — your next meeting, new mail, the song that's playing, a running timer — and opens into a panel when you want more.

  • Your dayYour next Google Calendar meeting with a one-click join link, and Classroom deadlines that are due soon.
  • Mail at a glanceUnread count and the latest senders from Gmail; archive or mark as read without opening a browser.
  • Media & SpotifyAlbum art, playback and lyrics for whatever is playing.
  • Focus & to-doTasks, timers, focus sessions, ambient sounds and break reminders.
  • Coding agentsLive status and approvals for Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI.
  • Private by designEverything runs on your PC. There is no Notch server and no account.

Google sign-in

"Continue with Google" is optional. It lets Notch Desktop show your Google Calendar events and, if you turn them on, your Gmail inbox summary and Google Classroom deadlines. Notch Desktop runs only on your computer: your Google data goes straight from Google to the app on your PC and is never sent to us. Read exactly what is accessed and why in the privacy policy.

Notch Desktop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Get it

Notch Desktop runs on Windows 10 and 11. Ask for the installer.
