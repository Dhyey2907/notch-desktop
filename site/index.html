Every part of your day, one bar.

Music & video

Everything playing, in its own colors.

Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video — whatever plays, Overline shows it with play, pause and skip. The panel takes the album art's colors (Netflix red, Prime blue) and the edge glows with the beat.

  • Hover the bar for a peek; click to open the player.
  • Time-synced lyrics and your pinned Spotify playlists.
  • Turn the glow off any time in Settings → Look.
Calendar

Never miss the start of a meeting.

A countdown appears before every meeting with a one-click Join. Add an event in seconds, and answer invites with Yes, Maybe or No — right from the top of your screen.

  • Connect Google Calendar in Settings → Calendar.
  • Choose how early the countdown starts.
  • Classroom deadlines show up in “Your day”.
Mail

Inbox zero, one swipe at a time.

Unread Gmail shows up one message at a time. Swipe left to archive, right to mark it read, or snooze it until tomorrow. Overline never sends or deletes mail.

  • Keys: A archive · R read · S snooze · → skip.
  • A small card for new mail, so you can stay in your work.
AI coding · Claude Code · Codex · GitHub

Your agents ask. You answer from the top.

When Claude Code or Codex needs permission, an Allow / Deny card drops down — no hunting for the right terminal. See your usage limits as rings, know when a long task finishes, and keep an eye on pull requests waiting for your review.

  • One click in Settings → AI connects Claude Code and Codex.
  • Questions from the agent still open in its own window.
  • Sign in to GitHub for reviews, checks and notifications.
Get things done

Type it once. It lands in the right place.

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere and type a line: “call mom at 5” becomes a reminder, “note: …” a note, “lunch with Ann friday 1pm” a calendar event, a link goes to Read later. To-dos get timers that ring, and saved links get a reading time and an AI summary.

  • Clipboard history with Ctrl+Alt+V, and a drop shelf for files.
  • Focus sessions with break reminders.
Your pet

A little friend who keeps you company.

Give it a name, a color and a personality — cheerful, sarcastic, calm, a coach… It dances to your music, reminds you to take a break, cheers your finished to-dos, and walks along the taskbar while you're away.

  • Click it for a little love; it levels up with you.
  • Choose exactly what it talks about in Settings → Pet.

Runs on your PC. Nothing else.

There is no Overline server and no account. Settings and history stay on your computer; passwords and sign-ins live in Windows Credential Manager. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Google sign-in

“Continue with Google” is optional. It lets Overline show your Google Calendar events and, if you turn them on, your Gmail inbox summary and Google Classroom deadlines, and — only if you allow it — add events and answer invites. Your Google data goes straight from Google to the app on your PC and is never sent to us. Read exactly what is accessed and why in the privacy policy.

Overline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
